How to play & the 17 modifiers

On your turn, tap a fish card in your hand to ask for that card's exact mate — same number AND same color. Tapping your 9♣ asks for the 9♠, the other black 9; tapping 9♥ asks for the 9♦, the other red 9. If they hold that one card, they hand over just that card — never every card of that number — and you ask again. If they don't have that exact card — Go Fish! — your pole casts into the whirlpool and hauls up 1 card. Fish your exact mate or pair up, and you go again. Starting pairs bank themselves. Most pairs wins when no more pairs can be made.

    Two different shields, two different jobs: Anchor ⚓ is anti-theft — it blocks the next Blind Snatch or Magnet Hook aimed at you, but not Storm Surge. Life Ring 🛟 is anti-disruption — it blocks the next Blind Snatch or Storm Surge, but not Magnet Hook. One modifier per turn.

    One action per turn: on your turn you either ask for one card or play one modifier — a modifier uses your whole turn, you don't get an ask after it. Hand limit: you can hold at most 3 modifiers. If you draw a 4th (a Go Fish draw or any draw effect), your oldest modifier goes straight back into the pond and the conversation strip says so. The limit covers your opening hand too — a 4th dealt modifier swaps back into the pond for a fish card before play starts, so nobody opens over the limit. Mods keep flowing: you're always dealt at least one modifier in your opening hand, whenever you draw from the pond while holding no modifiers you draw a modifier first, and if you start your turn holding none — or earn another turn — the pond deals you one (all while any are left in the pond) — so a mod is almost always in reach. Coin flip: who goes first is flipped fresh every game, rematches too — nobody gets a built-in head start. Mods that would help right now glow and pulse in your hand.

    The 17 — every one has a distinct job:
    🎣 Help you: Double Cast, Lucky Lure, Swap Bait, Goldie's Gift, Whirlpool Spin, Magnet Hook.
    💥 Hurt them: Blind Snatch, Storm Surge, Reel In, Bait & Switch.
    👁️ Reveal info: Peek, Mate Sense, Mirror Pond.
    🛟 Protect you: Anchor, Life Ring, Fog Bank.
    🎲 Wild: Pond Monster.
